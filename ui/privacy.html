Privacy policy

VidForge, the internal video studio behind the Hypercurious channels. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who uses VidForge

VidForge is a private tool used by a small team to make short educational videos and post them to the team's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When a team member connects a YouTube channel, VidForge asks Google for permission to upload videos, read the channel's basic details and video list, and read YouTube Analytics for that channel. It uses these only to post the team's own videos and to show view counts on the team dashboard.

What we store

  • A refresh token for the connected channel, kept in the team's private Supabase database and readable only by signed-in team members and the team's GitHub Actions worker.
  • Video titles, captions, IDs and public statistics (views, likes, comments) for the videos VidForge posted.

We do not collect data about viewers, do not sell or share any data, and do not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

Google user data

VidForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. VidForge uses the YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access

You can revoke VidForge's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. A team admin can also delete the stored token from VidForge Settings; it is removed from the database immediately.
