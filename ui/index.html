YouTube

Posts Shorts and videos to your channel.

How to get these
  1. In Google Cloud, enable YouTube Data API v3.
  2. Create an OAuth client of type Desktop app and download its JSON.
  3. On your laptop, in the repo: python run.py youtube-auth client_secret.json, sign in to the channel, copy the three values.
  4. Until Google audits your project, API uploads stay private. Apply for the audit early.

Instagram

Posts Reels to a professional (Creator or Business) account.

How to get these
  1. Switch the account to Professional in the Instagram app.
  2. Create a Meta app with "Instagram API with Instagram Login", add the account as a tester.
  3. Grant instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_content_publish, then generate a long-lived token. It lasts about 60 days.
